Methodology

No mysticism in the math. Here's exactly what we compute.

FACT — computed astronomy

  • Ephemeris: Swiss Ephemeris (Moshier model), sub-arcsecond planetary longitudes, computed locally — no third-party API.
  • Zodiac: Tropical (sign = season-anchored ecliptic longitude).
  • Houses: Whole-sign by default (each house is one whole sign from your rising sign); Placidus optional.
  • Retrograde (℞): shown when a planet's apparent longitudinal speed is negative — the literal definition, not a guess.
  • Aspects: angular separations within standard orbs (conjunction 8°, opposition 8°, square 7°, trine 7°, sextile 5°), each labelled applying or separating from relative motion, with an estimated time to exact.

OUR READ — interpretation

  • Energy / Focus / Friction and the flow score are heuristics we layer on top of the facts above. They are opinions, weighted from which houses each planet is transiting.
  • Chart ruler uses traditional (domicile) rulerships.
  • House one-word labels: {% for n, label in house_labels.items %}{{ n }} {{ label }}{% if not forloop.last %} · {% endif %}{% endfor %}.

Where you see OUR READ in the app, that's interpretation. Everything else is the sky as computed.
